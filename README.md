## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
```
1.Produce passes through multiple middlemen between farm and kitchen, and each one adds a margin.
2.Farmers stay with middlemen because middlemen give them same-day cash, credit, collection and transport.
3.in this current society,the farmers are abrubtly exploited by the middlemen.
```
## Proposed Solution
```
1.Technical support center will be allocated for registering farmer's crops for selling to the shops and then to public.
2.a set of centers are allocated for registration of selling crops through whatsapp for farmers.
3.Also there will be a webpage for registration for selling of crops online for farmers.
4.separate logistics team will be allocated by gatering the local logistics company as a community for transport from farmers to shop.
5.if the farmer andshop owner is local then the bull carts can be assigned to deliver it.
5.there will be points awarded for both farmer and shop owner which later give them additional discounts and perks.
6.by then the farmers get a profit,the shop owners will get a profit ,logistics team will get a profit and the people can get their goods in much cheaper manner itself.
```
